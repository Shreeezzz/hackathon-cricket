📋 Tournament format & rules

Venue: Ferns Basketball Court

27 August 2026 — Elimination Round: 16 teams, 8 matches. Each winner qualifies for the Quarter Finals.

28 August 2026 — Knockout Rounds: Quarter Finals (4) → Semi Finals (2) → Final.

Format is tentative and may be adjusted based on the final number of registered teams.

Team composition

6 players per team. Each innings limited to 6 overs.

Batting

Teams may decide their batting order strategically. A batsman cannot be retired or replaced unless dismissed. Batsmen may run between wickets after playing a shot.

Boundaries

Only the straight boundary counts for 4s and 6s. 4 runs if the ball reaches the boundary after bouncing; 6 runs if it clears on the full. Side boundaries, walls and netting count per the markings announced before the match.

2D rule

Any shot that hits or clears the second pole from the striker end (tentative) in the basketball court area is awarded 2D — 2 runs, as defined by the organizers before play.

Fielding

Maximum on the field at any time: 4 fielders, 1 wicket keeper, 1 bowler.

Catches

A direct catch off a full toss is out. If the ball directly strikes the overhead net without bouncing and is caught one-handed, the batsman is out.

Bowling

Underarm / short-arm deliveries only. 2 players may bowl a maximum of 2 overs each, and 2 additional players must bowl 1 over each. No bowler may exceed 2 overs.

Extras

Wides: two consecutive wides collectively count as 1 run.
No balls: any full toss passing above the batsman's waist height while standing upright at the popping crease is a No Ball.

Dismissals

LBW is not applicable and will not result in a dismissal.

Tie breaker

In the event of a tie, a Super Over is played — one over each, most runs wins.

Umpiring

The decision of the umpires is final and binding.
